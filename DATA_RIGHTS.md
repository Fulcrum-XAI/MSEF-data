# Dataset source and rights

The corpus is derived from the CCAMC ancient and modern Chinese character collection: <http://ccamc.org/cjkv_oaccgd.php>. The archive preserves source records, source URLs, bibliographic references, images, and cached pages for research reproducibility.

The upstream source files and third-party images retain their original rights. This repository does not claim ownership of, or grant a new blanket license for, third-party material. Please consult the source and the rights attached to each item before reuse or redistribution.

Counts describe the packaged snapshot and are not counts of independent characters, artifacts, or source images.
