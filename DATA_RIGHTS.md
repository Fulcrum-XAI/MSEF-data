# Dataset source and rights

The corpus is derived from the CCAMC ancient and modern Chinese character collection: <http://ccamc.org/cjkv_oaccgd.php>. It preserves source records, source URLs, bibliographic references, images, and cached source pages.

The upstream source files and third-party images retain their original rights and terms. This repository does not claim ownership of, or grant a new blanket license for, third-party material. Consult the source and the rights attached to each item before reuse or redistribution.

The archive contains the CCAMC source-corpus component only. It does not include the derived FGCCES correspondences, survival annotations, engineered feature files, or experiment splits. Counts describe the packaged snapshot and are not counts of independent characters, artifacts, or source images.
